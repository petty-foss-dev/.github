# Petty FOSS

Hi, I'm Isaac. I'm tired of people prompting out the same app we've all seen a hundred times, slapping a subscription on it, and acting like they invented something.

So I'm doing the petty version. I'll make the apps I want and give them away. Free and open source. Use them if they're useful. Fork them if you can do better. I'm just sick of being asked for eight bucks a month to use a glorified checklist.

And if somebody else already made a better free app, I'll link to theirs. The world doesn't need another shitty AI wrapper just because I could make one.

## Stuff I made

- **[petty: Tracker](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/Petty-Tracker):** Keep receipts, warranties, subscriptions, and documents on your device. iOS and Android.
- **[Petty Memory](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/Petty-Memory):** Keep links, notes, files, and decisions in one place. Share them with the AI tools you choose.
- **[Petty: Homelab](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/Petty-Homelab):** Keep an eye on Unraid and your self-hosted services from an iPhone or iPad.
- **[MemoryManager](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/MemoryManager):** See what's eating your Mac's memory, CPU, and storage.

All four projects are free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

If one of these Petty apps somehow takes off, I'll move it into my [Enve app ecosystem](https://envemedia.com) (the apps that I actually spend my time on and I've put tons of work into) and give it more time and attention. It'll still be free and open source forever. It just means people actually want it, so I'll put more work into it.

## Stuff other people made that's already good

I didn't make these. Go give their creators some credit.

### Money and paperwork

- **[Actual Budget](https://github.com/actualbudget/actual):** Budgeting that keeps your data on your device. The software is free; hosted options can cost money.
- **[InvoicePlane](https://github.com/InvoicePlane/InvoicePlane):** Make invoices and keep track of clients and payments. Self-hosted.
- **[PDF Arranger](https://github.com/pdfarranger/pdfarranger):** Merge, split, and rearrange PDFs on your computer.
- **[Reactive Resume](https://github.com/reactive-resume/reactive-resume):** Make a resume without paying a subscription.

### Notes, tasks, and everyday stuff

- **[Joplin](https://github.com/laurent22/joplin):** Notes and to-dos for desktop, iOS, and Android.
- **[Super Productivity](https://github.com/super-productivity/super-productivity):** Tasks, time tracking, and focus timers.
- **[Mealie](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie):** Recipes, meal plans, and shopping lists. You host it yourself.
- **[Loop Habit Tracker](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits):** Track habits on Android, offline and without ads.

### Workouts

- **[wger](https://github.com/wger-project/wger):** Track workouts and nutrition. You can host it yourself.
- **[Skulpt](https://github.com/skulptapp/skulpt):** Plan and log workouts on iOS, Android, and Apple Watch.

### AI and quick tools

- **[Yes-Brainer](https://github.com/trekhleb/yesbrainer):** Bounce an idea off several AI models. Bring your own API keys or run Ollama locally. The app is free; model providers may charge you.
- **[QR Code Generator](https://github.com/xhluca/qr-code):** Make a static QR code without an account or subscription. The destination is baked in, so you can't change it after printing.

### Websites and small businesses

- **[HeyForm](https://github.com/heyform/heyform):** Make forms and surveys. Host it yourself instead of paying for another form builder.
- **[LittleLink](https://github.com/sethcottle/littlelink):** Put your links on one page you control. It needs a bit of HTML editing and somewhere to host it.
- **[Shlink](https://github.com/shlinkio/shlink):** Short links and redirects on your own domain. You run the server.
- **[Umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami):** See your website traffic without handing it to an analytics SaaS. Self-hosted.
- **[Easy!Appointments](https://github.com/alextselegidis/easyappointments):** Let people book appointments through a service you host yourself.

### Tabletop RPGs

- **[Esiana](https://github.com/Esiana-ttrpg/esiana-core):** Run D&D and other tabletop campaigns with a self-hosted world wiki, maps, timelines, and player discoveries.

## Books, audiobooks, and comics

- **[AudioBooth](https://github.com/AudioBooth/AudioBooth):** Listen to audiobooks and read EPUBs or PDFs from Audiobookshelf on iOS. Free and open source, made by the AudioBooth developer.
- **[Enve Book Player](https://github.com/opisaac9001/Enve-Book-Player):** My separate Enve project for audiobooks, ebooks, comics, and podcasts on iOS and Android. Free and open source under AGPL-3.0-only.
- **[Absorb](https://github.com/pounat/absorb):** A free, open-source Audiobookshelf player for Android and iOS. Made by Nathan Poulson.
- **[Grimmory](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory):** Host a library of ebooks, comics, and audiobooks, with a browser reader and OPDS support.
- **[Storyteller](https://gitlab.com/storyteller-platform/storyteller):** Sync an ebook with its audiobook so you can read and listen along together. Self-hosted.
