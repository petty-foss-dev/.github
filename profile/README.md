# Petty FOSS

Hi, I'm the developer behind Petty FOSS. If you're like me, you're tired of seeing a vibe-coded SaaS thrown together in an afternoon, wrapped in a subscription, and sold like a breakthrough.

So I decided to be petty: I'm building useful alternatives and making them free and open source. Use them, fork them, improve them, or ignore them. I just think useful software deserves better than a quick-buck paywall.

## The apps

- **[petty: Tracker](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/Petty-Tracker)** — Track receipts, warranties, subscriptions, and documents on your device. iOS and Android.
- **[Petty Memory](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/Petty-Memory)** — Save links, notes, files, and decisions in a local-first library shared with the AI tools you choose to use.
- **[Petty: Homelab](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/Petty-Homelab)** — Monitor Unraid and self-hosted services, and handle routine maintenance from iPhone or iPad.
- **[MemoryManager](https://github.com/petty-foss-dev/MemoryManager)** — Monitor memory, CPU, and storage on macOS and take action before memory pressure becomes a problem.

All four projects are free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Good software by other people

If someone else has already built a useful free and open-source app, we'd rather point you to it than reinvent it. These projects are made and maintained by their respective creators; Petty FOSS is just recommending them.

- **[Actual Budget](https://github.com/actualbudget/actual)** — Local-first personal budgeting. Free to self-host; managed hosting may cost money.
- **[Joplin](https://github.com/laurent22/joplin)** — Notes and to-dos across desktop, iOS, and Android, with optional sync.
- **[Super Productivity](https://github.com/super-productivity/super-productivity)** — Tasks, time tracking, and focus timers.
- **[Mealie](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie)** — Self-hosted recipes, meal plans, and shopping lists.
- **[PDF Arranger](https://github.com/pdfarranger/pdfarranger)** — Merge, split, rotate, crop, and rearrange PDF pages on desktop.
- **[Loop Habit Tracker](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits)** — An offline, ad-free habit tracker for Android.
- **[wger](https://github.com/wger-project/wger)** — Workout and nutrition tracking you can self-host.
- **[Reactive Resume](https://github.com/reactive-resume/reactive-resume)** — Build and export resumes without a subscription.
- **[Skulpt](https://github.com/skulptapp/skulpt)** — Local-first workout planning and logging for iOS, Android, and Apple Watch. Optional sync.
- **[Yes-Brainer](https://github.com/trekhleb/yesbrainer)** — Bounce ideas off several AI models using your own API keys or a local Ollama model. The app has no subscription; cloud model providers may charge for usage.
